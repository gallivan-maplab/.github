# Memory, Action & Perception Laboratory (MAPlab)

**Queen's University · Kingston, Ontario, Canada**  
Centre for Neuroscience Studies · Department of Psychology · Department of Biomedical & Molecular Sciences  
**Principal Investigator:** Jason P. Gallivan, PhD (`gallivan@queensu.ca`)

[Lab Website](https://www.gallivanmaplab.com/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=z4zspOMAAAAJ&hl=en) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7362-109X) · [OpenNeuro Dataset ds005598](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds005598)

At **MAPlab**, we combine functional and structural 3T MRI, low-dimensional neural manifold modelling, and robotic psychophysics to investigate how distributed cortical, subcortical, and cerebellar networks learn, remember, perceive, and guide skilled human action.

## Open Neuroimaging & Behavioural Datasets

- **OpenNeuro `ds005598` — Visuomotor Rotation & Reward-Based Motor Learning fMRI** ([Dataset](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds005598) · `doi:10.18112/openneuro.ds005598.v1.0.3`): Whole-brain T1w, resting-state, right-hand visuomotor rotation adaptation, reward-based motor learning, and left-hand intermanual transfer fMRI and behavioural recordings (CC0).
- **Motor Savings Source Data Archive** ([Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18613054)): Underlying numerical and manifold eccentricity data accompanying Rezaei et al. (2026, *PLOS Biology*).
